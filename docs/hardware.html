IMU sample rates

Fitted part is LSM6DSVQTR ×2, IIO st,lsm6dsv. SPI mode 3, spi-max-frequency 1 MHz, controller max-freq 10 MHz. DTB: st,odr-triggered, DRDY on INT1. HUD writes sampling_frequency (oneshot sysfs).
