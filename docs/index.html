Global shutter on this platform

EGO is a stereo MIPI board. Both eyes share one SoC, one pair of CSI PHYs, and one hardware tick. SKUs on this PCB: SC233HGS, OG02C10, AM220. Other global-shutter imagers from the Camemake catalogue fit the same two-camera footprint: SmartSens, OmniVision, onsemi and Sony Pregius. Colour, mono and NIR.

Every option on this platform is a global-shutter MIPI module. Address on each bus is 0x30. CAM0 is I2C3; CAM1 is bit-banged I2C (SCL/SDA swapped on the PCB).
