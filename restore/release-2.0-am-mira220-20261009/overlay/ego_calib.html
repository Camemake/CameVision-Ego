While recording

  1. Hold the board upright in your hands, facing the glasses. Do not lean it on the floor.
  2. Stand ~1.00 m away. The whole 11×8 grid must fill most of both live images. The NPU searches the whole frame.
  3. Wait for magenta dots / LOCKED. Then slide the board into the pink cell.
  4. A little tilt is good. Avoid window glare on the squares.
  5. When center + 4 corners are green, press Stop & save.

Set the lens

  1. Hold the chart upright, parallel to the glasses, at 50 cm.
  2. Fill most of both live images with the black frame.
  3. When the chart is seen, the overlay says ISO 12233 and a sharpness number appears.
  4. Loosen the M12 lock ring. Turn the lens until that number peaks, then lock.
  5. Repeat for the other eye.
